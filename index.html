Self-introduction (60–90 sec) — read this first

“Good morning, sir/ma’am. I’m Janie Joy Abutal, a BS Psychology graduate of Central Mindanao University and a Registered Psychometrician since August 2024. I’m from Bukidnon, and it’s an honor to be considered for this position.

My most meaningful experiences have been with people. At Journey Homes Wellness Center, I supported clients in recovery — building trust without judgment and documenting their behavioral progress for the clinical team. With the Red Cross Youth Council, I served in community health and disaster preparedness campaigns. And in my HR internship at SEDA Centrio, I handled confidential records for over a hundred employees and built a digital filing system that’s still a point of pride for me.

After my licensure, I worked remotely — first in market research, then as a virtual assistant — and I did well in both. But I realized that remote work, comfortable as it was, didn’t fulfill me. I wasn’t connected to anyone; nothing I did reached an actual person. So I resigned from the research role and used the VA work to sustain myself while deliberately searching for the work my heart was actually craving: helping people in need, face to face.

That search led me here. This position brings together everything I have — my psychology training, my documentation and coordination skills, and my experience with clients in recovery — in service of communities in our own province. I’m not here by accident, sir/ma’am. I’m here on purpose.”
